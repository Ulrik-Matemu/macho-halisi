Two million animals move north with the rain. For eight days you move with them — no fixed script, no convoy, just your guide reading the plains each morning and deciding where the day should go.

You begin in the Seronera valley while the herds are still gathering, then work north through the western corridor as the columns thicken. By day six you are on the Mara River, waiting for a crossing with nothing but the sound of hooves building somewhere out of sight.

The journey closes on the floor of the Ngorongoro Crater — a full day inside a collapsed volcano holding the densest concentration of wildlife on the continent.

01 — 02Arusha · Seronera

Arrival, then the first plains

Met at Kilimanjaro airport and driven to a garden lodge outside Arusha for the night. In the morning, a light aircraft drops you on a grass strip in the Seronera valley — your guide is waiting at the wing, and the first game drive begins on the way to camp.

Legendary Serengeti CampFull board
03 — 05Western corridor

Moving with the herds

Three days shaped entirely by where the animals are. Long morning drives, lunch under an acacia, and afternoons spent with whichever pride, cheetah or column of wildebeest the day gives you. One dawn is reserved for a balloon flight over the plains, ending with breakfast on the grass.

06Mara River

The crossing

North to the river, where the herds gather on the bank and wait — sometimes for an hour, sometimes for a day. Your guide picks a vantage point and you hold it. When the first animal commits, the rest follow in a single unbroken line.

Nomad LamaiFull-day drive
07 — 08Ngorongoro · Departure

Down into the crater

An early descent through cloud onto the crater floor: black rhino in the marsh, lion on the short grass, flamingo along the soda lake. A last night on the rim, then the road back to Arusha and your onward flight.
